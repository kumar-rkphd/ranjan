Research Focus & Core Themes

Primary areas of hydroclimate and atmospheric research

Atmospheric Moisture Budget & Transport

Decomposing vertically integrated moisture flux divergence into mean ($M1$) and transient eddy ($M4$) components to analyze water availability ($E-P$) across agro-climatic zones.

Lagrangian Moisture Tracking & Extremes

Utilizing Lagrangian particle dispersion models (UTrack & HYSPLIT) to trace local vs. remote evaporative moisture sources for extreme precipitation and atmospheric rivers.

Land-Atmosphere Interactions & GIS Suitability

Evaluating soil-moisture–ET–precipitation feedbacks and applying multi-criteria GIS & remote sensing models to map agricultural land suitability under climate change.
